# ROARemoteTrigger — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A Wrath 3.3.5 action-slot roleplay emote system with per-slot settings, profiles and recruitment messaging.

## Design identity

Triggers follow player key/button intent, including failed casts, rather than combat success; it is explicitly flavor rather than rotation automation.

Retrospective synthesis from the existing README, source and addon metadata.
